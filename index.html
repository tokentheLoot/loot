About LOOT

theLoot (LOOT) is an experimental ERC-20 token exploring decentralized value creation. It aims to enable crypto payments between users and merchants with low friction and full transparency.

Commerce Usage

Merchants can accept LOOT via Web3 wallets and QR codes, and instantly swap to stablecoins (e.g., USDT) on Uniswap to manage volatility while keeping liquidity.
